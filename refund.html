Refund Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Refund Eligibility

We offer refunds under specific circumstances. Please read the following conditions carefully:

Important: Refunds are processed to your account balance, not to the original payment method.

When Refunds Are Applicable

  • Order Not Started: If your order has not been started within 24 hours
  • Service Unavailable: If the service becomes unavailable after payment
  • Partial Delivery: Refund for the undelivered portion of the order
  • Wrong Link: If you provided the correct link but we processed the wrong one

When Refunds Are NOT Applicable

  • Orders that have been fully completed
  • Drops or decreases after order completion (this is normal)
  • If you provided an incorrect link
  • Private or restricted accounts
  • Account suspension by the platform
  • Services that are marked as "Non-Refundable"

Refund Processing Time

Refund requests are typically processed within 24-48 hours. The refunded amount will be added to your account balance automatically.

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact our support team with:

  • Your Order ID
  • Reason for refund request
  • Any supporting screenshots or evidence

Contact Support

For refund requests or any questions about our refund policy, please reach out through our WhatsApp or Telegram support channels.
